ISNAD - Privacy Policy

Last updated: 31 October 2026

Summary

ISNAD does not collect, store on any server, share or sell any personal data. It has no account, no ads, no analytics and no tracking.

What stays on your phone

  • Location: used only when you tap "Use my location", to calculate prayer times and the Qibla direction. The coordinates are saved in the app's local storage on your phone and are never sent anywhere. You can instead choose a city from the list.
  • Settings: city, calculation method, reminder choices, text size, language, mosque times, and any Adhan audio file you pick. All are stored locally on your phone.
  • Notifications: prayer reminders are scheduled on your phone by Android. The "alarms and reminders" permission is used only so that reminders sound at the right minute.
  • Compass sensor: the Qibla compass reads your phone's orientation sensor on the device only.

Network

The app works offline. It does not contact any server to show prayer times or duas. Links in the About page (for example tanzil.net) open in your browser, which is governed by that website's policy.

Children

The app collects no data from anyone, including children.

Deleting your data

Clear the app's storage in Android Settings, or uninstall the app, and everything is removed.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published at this address with a new date.

## Full description (English)
ISNAD is a simple, ad-free Islamic companion for Muslims everywhere, built on authentic (Sahih) hadith.

- Prayer times anywhere in the world, by GPS or by searching your city (works offline)
- Adhan and reminders at prayer time, set as Android alarms so they work when the app is closed; choose from several Adhan voices
- Qibla compass, Hijri date, Tahajjud, Duha and forbidden times
- Morning, evening and night adhkar with counters, and a tasbih counter
- Ramadan mode (suhoor and iftar), reminders for sunnah fasts and Friday
- Prayer log with streaks, kept only on your phone
- Step-by-step prayer guide: Adhan, wudu, Iqamah, the prayer, Witr, travel prayer, Jumma, Eid, Janazah and Taraweeh, with hadith references
- Match your own mosque timetable with fixed times or minute adjustments
- English and Tamil, three text sizes, dark mode
- No account, no ads, no tracking. Your location never leaves your phone.

Prayer times are calculated and may differ from your mosque by a minute or two. For rulings, consult a qualified scholar.

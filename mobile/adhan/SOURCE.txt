Adhan audio - source and licence note
Recordings come from https://github.com/rehanhaider/adhan (media/Adhan-Makkah1, Adhan-Madinah, Adhan-Makkah2, Adhan-fajr), a fork of https://github.com/achaudhry/adhan.
That repository's code is MIT licensed, but it does not say who recorded the audio or under what licence, so the rights are NOT verified.
Each file is the first 75 seconds (mono, 56 kbps, fade-out) so it plays fully as an Android notification sound.
Replace with recordings you have the right to use before publishing the app. To add a voice: put the mp3 here and add it to voices.json.
